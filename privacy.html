Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

Gauge Scout ("the App") is developed by Conn River Design. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data We Do Not Collect

Gauge Scout does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not have user accounts or login systems.

Data Sources

The App displays publicly available data from the following sources:

  • U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) — real-time streamflow and gauge data
  • National Weather Prediction Service (NWPS) — hydrological forecasts
  • Apple WeatherKit — weather forecasts and precipitation data (Pro subscribers only)
  • NOAA MRMS — radar-based rainfall estimates
  • American Whitewater — river reach information and links

All data is fetched directly from these public APIs to your device. No data passes through our servers.

On-Device Processing

Flow predictions are generated entirely on your device using bundled machine-learning models. No gauge data, location data, or usage data is transmitted off-device for this purpose.

Subscriptions

Gauge Scout Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription managed entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not process or store any payment information. Subscription status is verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit framework.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are generated locally on your device based on flow conditions. No push notification server is used.

Data Storage

Your gauge preferences, alert ranges, and cached data are stored locally on your device. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@connriverdesign.com.
